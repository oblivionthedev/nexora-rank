# Nexora Rank Acceptable Use Policy

_Last updated: August 27, 2026 · Pre-launch draft_

Do not use Nexora to harass or endanger users; automate spam or raids; evade Discord or Roblox enforcement, permissions, or rate limits; request or share user tokens, security cookies, passwords, or private keys; falsify activity, identities, rank eligibility, or audit evidence; access unauthorized accounts or workspaces; broker platform-derived personal data; or distribute fraud, malware, unlawful content, or intellectual-property infringement.

Nexora may slow, block, reverse, preserve evidence of, or report abusive activity where reasonably necessary. Serious or repeated violations may lead to suspension or termination. A dedicated abuse and security channel will be published before launch.
