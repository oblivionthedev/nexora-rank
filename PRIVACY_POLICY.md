# Nexora Rank Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 27, 2026 · Pre-launch draft_

This policy describes the intended production data flow. Operator identity and contact details will be finalized before launch.

## Data processed

Nexora may process Discord identifiers, usernames, avatars, server IDs, roles, and authorized membership; Roblox identifiers, usernames, display names, group membership, ranks, and authorized Open Cloud results; workspace configuration, applications, activity summaries, approvals, automation events, API-key metadata, webhooks, and audit records; technical/security logs; and billing identifiers after payments launch. Full card details remain with the checkout provider.

## Purpose and legal basis

Data is used to provide linking and workspace features, secure the service, prevent abuse, maintain auditability, support users, and meet legal obligations. Applicable legal bases may include contract, legitimate interests, consent, or legal obligation.

## Young users

Nexora is not directed to children under 13. Where consent is required, users below their country's digital-consent age require parent or guardian consent. Community owners must not collect unnecessary personal information from minors.

## Sharing

Data may be shared with infrastructure, authentication, monitoring, support, and future payment providers only as needed. Discord and Roblox process requests as independent platforms. Nexora does not sell personal data or use Roblox API data to train AI models.

## Retention and rights

Data is retained while needed for service, security, audit, and legal purposes. Users may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability where applicable; revoke Discord or Roblox access; withdraw consent; or complain to the relevant authority.

## Security and transfers

Nexora is designed around scoped OAuth, row-level access controls, encrypted transport, secret isolation, and auditable privileged operations. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Appropriate transfer safeguards will be used where required.

## Contact

A dedicated privacy email and legal operator identity will be published before public registration. Until then, do not submit sensitive information through the preview.
